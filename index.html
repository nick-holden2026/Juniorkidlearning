🔐 Parent Corner – Grown-ups Only

Learning progress is stored in this browser on this device. No account is required and nothing is sent anywhere.

Learning tip: After every lesson, show your child the real computer part and encourage her to say its English name.

How stages unlock: each stage ends with a Boss Quiz. Four right answers out of five opens the next stage. The quiz shuffles its answers and can be retried.

About Stages 3 to 5: they teach the ideas (patterns, learning from examples, predicting the next word) through short lessons and quizzes. For hands-on practice later, try Scratch, then Google's Teachable Machine, then Python.

Please supervise computer use, take regular breaks and practise with real computer equipment.
